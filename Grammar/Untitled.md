## Questions to ask the Enrolments Office

1. **My child is currently in Year 3. Based on their date of birth, which entry year should we apply for if we are considering Year 5 entry and Year 7 entry?**
2. **Are Year 5 and Year 7 the main intake years for your school?**
3. **For a current Year 3 student, are we still able to apply for Year 5 entry, or would the application go onto a waiting list?**
4. **If we apply now for Year 7 entry, would my child be placed on the Enrolment List or the Waiting List?**
5. **Can we apply for both Year 5 and Year 7 entry at the same time, or do we need to submit separate applications?**
6. **If we apply for Year 5 but do not receive an offer, can the same application still be considered for Year 7 entry?**
7. **What is the best strategy for our situation: apply for Year 5 now, apply for Year 7 now, or apply for both?**
8. **What documents do we need to provide at the application stage?**
9. **When will school reports, NAPLAN results, learning support documents, and co-curricular information be required?**
10. **Is there an interview, assessment, entrance test, or academic review as part of the enrolment process?**
11. **How are waiting list applications reviewed? Is the waiting list based on application date, student profile, availability, or other factors?**
12. **When are offers usually made for Year 5 and Year 7 entry?**
13. **What are the application fee, confirmation fee, and any other enrolment-related fees? Are these fees refundable?**
14. **If we receive a Year 5 offer and accept it, does that automatically secure continuation into Year 7?**
15. **Are there scholarships or bursaries available for Year 5 or Year 7 entry? If so, when should we apply?**
16. **Is there anything we can do now to strengthen our child’s application or improve their chance of receiving an offer?**
